# HR_Analytics_PowerBi_Dashboard

## HR_Analytics_Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard that summarizes employee headcount, demographics, and attrition. Department and age-group slicers help users compare workforce patterns across employee groups.

## Project overview
In this project, I analyzed employee data to gain meaningful insights into employee demographics, attrition, departments, salary, job roles, experience, and job satisfaction. The goal of this project was to analyze employee data and understand the key factors associated with employee attrition and workforce distribution, helping HR teams make more data-driven decisions.

## Key KPIs Created:

- Total Employees: 1,470
- Active Employees: Over 1K
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Average Age: 36.92 years
- Average Experience: 7.01 years

## Key Analysis Performed:

- Analyzed which departments have the highest employee attrition.
- Explored the relationship between salary ranges and employee attrition.
- Compared attrition patterns between male and female employees.
- Analyzed the distribution of employees across different age groups.
- Examined how employee experience is related to attrition.
- Compared workforce distribution across different departments.
- Created a detailed matrix to analyze job satisfaction levels across different job roles.
